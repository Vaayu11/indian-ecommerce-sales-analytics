# Indian E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Indian e-commerce sales performance from January 2025 to June 2026 using Tableau. The objective is to transform sales and order data into actionable business insights that support better decision-making.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales trends, product category performance, customer segments, order fulfilment, and key business performance indicators.

## 2. Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer five key business questions:

- How does sales performance change over time?
- Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which customer segments generate the highest sales?
- What proportion of orders are successfully delivered?
- What business actions could improve growth, customer retention, and order fulfilment?

## 3. Tools and Technologies

## 5. Key Findings

### Finding 1: Product Performance

Electronics is the leading sales category, contributing approximately ₹3,094.6M in sales, as displayed in the dashboard.

**Business implication:** Investigate inventory availability, pricing, and margins to identify opportunities for profitable growth.

### Finding 2: Customer Segments

The Platinum customer tier contributes approximately ₹3,841.4M in sales, according to the dashboard.

**Business implication:** Analyze purchasing frequency, customer behavior, and repeat-order patterns to identify opportunities to improve retention among high-value customers.

### Finding 3: Order Operations

Approximately 80.2% of orders are delivered. The dashboard also reports 8,842 cancellations and 8,940 returns.

**Business implication:** Investigate cancellation and return reasons by product, city, and payment method to identify opportunities to improve order fulfilment and customer experience.

## 6. Business Recommendations

1. **Product Growth:** Review Electronics inventory, pricing, and margins to identify opportunities for profitable growth.
2. **Customer Retention:** Analyze Platinum customer purchasing behavior and develop targeted retention initiatives.
3. **Order Fulfilment:** Investigate cancellations and returns to identify operational bottlenecks and improve delivery performance.

## 7. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to organize sales data, identify business patterns, and communicate actionable recommendations.

The analysis connects product performance, customer value, and order fulfilment to practical business decisions rather than focusing solely on visualization.

## 8. Limitations and Further Analysis

The findings are descriptive and identify areas for further investigation. Additional analysis would be required to establish the causes of observed patterns and quantify the impact of proposed interventions.

Potential extensions include customer cohort analysis, repeat-purchase analysis, profitability analysis, and deeper investigation of cancellation and return drivers.
